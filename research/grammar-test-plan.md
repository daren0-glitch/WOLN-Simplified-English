# Grammar understanding test plan

**Status** · NOT RUN

## Questions and material

Measure meaning preservation in familiar-vocabulary WOLN with and without the specification. Measure people and AI separately. Construct a fresh, versioned set of short statements, questions and instructions. Keep abbreviations fully spelled out for the grammar-only test. Use matched Standard English controls for comparable meaning and difficulty.

Include exact quantity, unnumbered base nouns, time, ongoing and completed action, participants, negation and modality, conditions, direction, comparison and referent. Include intentionally underspecified items and a keyed set of acceptable neutral or conditional readings. Track the G01–G60 rules covered without treating proposals as approved.

## Run

1. Freeze test messages, semantic anchors and acceptable variants before collecting responses. Keep keys separate and exclude the archived pilot and its paraphrases from the new set.
2. Give the blind AI group only the instructions and test items. Give the context-assisted group the instructions, test items and [WOLN context](../WOLN_CONTEXT.md) without the key. Use fresh isolated sessions, consistent settings and counterbalanced matched forms or random assignment. Record model, version, date, tools, condition, prompt and verbatim outputs.
3. For human comprehension, recruit consenting participants across first languages and English proficiency. Randomly assign exposure or counterbalance matched forms. Ask for a plain-language paraphrase and targeted questions. Record comprehension and optional response time, and report group sizes and proficiency.
4. Have two reviewers who are blind to condition score each answer with the fixed key. Use one point per preserved anchor. Separately flag unsupported additions, contradictions and appropriate clarifications. Resolve disagreements through documented adjudication and report agreement. Keep model confidence separate.
5. Report counts and denominators, mean or median scores by condition, error patterns by rule, uncertainty and limits. Preserve original answers and keys with each dated result. Use consented or de-identified material in any public report.

## Example scoring principle

For an unnumbered base noun, accept a neutral paraphrase or an explicit note that quantity is unspecified. An answer that introduces an exact quantity gets an unsupported-detail flag. For a numbered noun, score the exact stated quantity. Apply equivalent treatment to unstated recipients, time and completion.
