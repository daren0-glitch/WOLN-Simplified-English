## Observations and scoring questions

- The GPT-6 pilot generally interpreted ordinary simplified patterns and recognized familiar abbreviations such as `tmr`, `yday` and `msg`.
- Item 18 recorded uncertainty about `Alyz` and `cmp`. The original key included “composition” as a tentative reading. Current draft guidance leaves `cmp` unresolved and records `CMPSTN` only as a proposal.
- Item 19 showed a possible collision with the established year-to-date meaning of `YTD`.
- Item 10's source, `3 child play game in park yday.`, explicitly counts the children and leaves the number of games open. The historic key expects “games” and the original paraphrase says “a game.” Review the plural-only anchor and the paraphrase's extra singular specificity independently. The published 98/100 remains the original self-grade.
- Item 11 adds a recipient and selects a past tense while flagging uncertainty. Item 17 leans toward a future reading despite ambiguous timing. Future rubrics should record added facts separately. The risk extends to added quantities, such as changing an unnumbered noun into an exact count.

## Next validation

Use [fresh grammar items](grammar-test-plan.md) and [separate short-word items](short-words-test-plan.md). Compare blind and context-assisted conditions with independent scoring. Run human comprehension and [speech](../docs/spoken-language.md) separately. This pilot supports observations about one small sample and provides no estimate of global human comprehension.
