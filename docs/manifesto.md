# WOLN manifesto

## WOLN Simplified English

We use familiar English word and simple grammar. Write first. Read first. Speak naturally. Any word can have short form, but full word always welcome. AI search and suggest. People choose, share and improve. Community use help language grow. Keep meaning clear.

## Standard English

WOLN welcomes familiar English words and simpler grammar. We begin with writing and reading, while speech develops naturally through research and use. Any word may have a short form, and full English remains welcome. AI searches and suggests. People choose, share and improve forms. Communities shape common usage. Clear meaning comes first.

## 繁體中文

WOLN 使用熟悉的英文詞彙和簡化文法。先書寫、先閱讀，口語隨研究與使用自然發展。任何單字都可以提出短形式，完整英文始終受到歡迎。AI 協助搜尋與建議，人們自由選擇、分享及改善，社群透過實際使用發展共同形式。清楚表達意思最重要。
