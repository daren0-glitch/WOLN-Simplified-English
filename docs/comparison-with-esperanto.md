# WOLN and Esperanto

| Dimension | Esperanto | WOLN V1.0 Draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | Constructed international auxiliary language first published in 1887. | Simplified English proposal for human and AI communication. |
| Vocabulary | Developed from roots drawn primarily from European languages. | Starts from familiar English, including full words and optionally short written forms. |
| Grammar | Uses a deliberately regular grammar and word formation system. | Uses recognizable English word order and makes many English inflections optional where meaning permits. |
| Speech | Has established spelling and pronunciation conventions. | Accepts full English pronunciation. Short spoken forms are a research objective. |
| Community | Has a documented history of use and development. | Is an open research draft gathering feedback and validation evidence. |

WOLN draws on Esperanto's regularity and community development. Its starting hypothesis is that people who know basic English may be able to use WOLN with little additional learning. Comparative comprehension and learning studies would be needed to measure that hypothesis across language backgrounds.
