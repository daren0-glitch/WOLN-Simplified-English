# WOLN Core Grammar V0.9

**Source** · V0.9 Revision 2  
**Review status** · GRAMMAR REVIEW — PARTIALLY APPROVED

The [complete English specification](grammar-v0.9-revision-2.md) and [Traditional Chinese source](grammar-v0.9-revision-2.zh-TW.md) contain G01–G60 and their review decisions. The V1.0 Draft documentation package cites that revision as its grammar baseline. Proposed and revised rows retain their recorded status.

| Area | Reference | Working guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sentence structure | G01–G06 | Keep familiar SVO order. Short sentences and clear subject omission in chat remain review proposals. |
| Nouns and articles | G07–G16 | Keep singular base nouns with exact counts, such as `1 app`, `2 app`, `3 child` and `100 file`. Keep the final letter in words such as `bus` and `news`. Articles are optional. Clarify specific references with `this`, `that`, names or context. |
| Verbs and time | G17–G28 | Default to base verbs and express relevant time explicitly. Retain familiar optional forms, including `be`, `-ing` and passive voice. The perfect-aspect guidance in G21 remains proposed. |
| Questions and negation | G29–G34 | Keep question words and negation. A question with statement order remains a proposal in G29. `Don't` plus the base verb is the approved simplified negation. Preserve meanings carried by `no`, `not` and `can't`. |
| Comparison and prepositions | G35–G42 | Allow `more` and `most`, and keep familiar forms like `better` and `faster`. Flexible `at/in/on` applies when the relation stays clear. Preserve direction and physical distinctions. |
| Complex sentences | G43–G50 | English connectors, modals, relative clauses and reporting forms remain compatible. Preserve conditions, participants and important time relations when simplifying. |
| Compatibility | G51–G60 | Full English words and Standard English remain compatible. Clarify ambiguous references and validate speech separately. |

Examples with `-ing` include `She work now.`, `She working now.`, and `She working.` when the time is clear from context. `She working yday at 9.` places an ongoing action in the past. `She was happy yesterday.` and `She happy yday.` are both accepted.

`Book in box.` and `Book on box.` describe different locations. `You like coffee?` and `What you buy yday?` illustrate proposed question forms. Keep the source's uncertainty about the actor, recipient, completion or time when those details are unstated.
